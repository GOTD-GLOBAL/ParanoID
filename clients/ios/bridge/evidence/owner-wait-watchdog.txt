  250 ms), then samples a MainActor-local flag. This asserts an unspecified
  scheduler latency, not just bounded completion. Both flag access and completion
  are MainActor-isolated; no data race was found. Deadline cancellation failure
  was a competing hypothesis, not assumed away. Exact Mac scheduling cause still
  needs the native rerun; controlled host latency demonstrates the brittle oracle.
- Replace the flag/sample with XCTestExpectation fulfilled in the caller's defer
  and async static XCTWaiter.fulfillment, timeout 5 s. It yields the MainActor
  and does not transfer the non-Sendable XCTestCase instance. Swift 6.3.3 strict
  concurrency / warnings-as-errors compilation and execution pass on Linux.

  Deadline remains 1 s; cancel/task/close retain 30 s deadlines, so a broken
  cancellation cannot pass by waiting for those deadlines. Success returns early.
  GateVerifier stays held until the finite assertion, then is released even on
  RED (also protected by defer). task.value is awaited only AFTER release.
  All four controls, rejection of late success, unchanged backend events, zero
  effects and valid successor checks are retained without weakening assertions.
