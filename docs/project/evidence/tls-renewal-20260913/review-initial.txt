Read-only review of the script, AGENTS, requested deployment/security/access canon and ADR-0003. **Three blockers:**

- **Recovery CLI requires an already healthy service** (117–124). After interruption following certificate replacement or during restart, `rollback` can fail at `health()` before restoring anything. `apply` cannot resume when the new certificate is installed, and an existing `apply-started.json` prevents retry after automatic recovery. **Fix:** provide state-aware recovery under the same lock, accepting only the recorded old/new certificate hashes, without requiring initial health. Preserve journal evidence while allowing explicit resume/recovery; verify health and exact served certificate afterward.

- **Forward and recovery writes share one exclusive temporary pathname** (52–55, 149). Failure/interruption after creating `.renewal-pending` but before rename leaves it present; the compensating write then necessarily fails with `EEXIST`. Marking `changed=True` early does not solve this. **Fix:** use distinct exclusively created temporary files for recovery, or explicitly reconcile a validated owned pending file. Exercise interruption before/after rename and directory-fsync failure.

- **Restart completion is mistaken for application readiness** (130–132, 150–151). The repository unit starts a supervisor that subsequently launches PostgreSQL/server; immediate health can race startup and falsely trigger rollback, then falsely report recovery failure. A timed-out `systemctl restart` also does not cancel the manager’s queued job. **Fix:** bounded readiness polling, including exact served DER; reconcile outstanding unit jobs before compensating restart. Test delayed startup and restart timeout.
